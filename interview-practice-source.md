# Henry Mao：外商設計主管面試英文回答練習 30 題

應徵方向：Senior Industrial Designer / Senior Product Designer

英文程度：用字以美國高中程度為主，保留必要的設計專業詞彙。每題約可回答 30–60 秒。

本稿依英文履歷撰寫。專案回答區分自己的貢獻與團隊成果。求職動機、工作習慣、自我評估及未來計畫是建議回答，請依真實情況調整。履歷沒有記載的衝突、失敗細節與數據不另行編造。

練習時先記住「主要觀點、案例、學到的事」，再用自己的話表達。描述個人工作用 I，描述團隊決定及成果用 we。不要把整個專案的功勞都放在自己身上。

## 01. Tell me about yourself.

**中文題意：請介紹一下自己。**

**英文回答**

I'm Henry Mao, an industrial and product designer with over ten years of experience. I've worked on computers, mice, bags, gaming chairs, and mobile accessories at ASUS. My strength is taking a design from an early idea through testing and into production. For example, I worked on the ASUS fragrance mouse and the ROG ERGO AIR backpack. I also led the ARCHER backpack project. I'm looking for a senior design role where I can stay involved in design and help the team solve product problems.

**練習提示：** 順序是背景、強項、兩個案例、求職方向。不必列出所有產品。最後一句請確認符合目前目標。

## 02. Why are you interested in this role and our company?

**中文題意：為什麼對這個職位和我們公司有興趣？**

**英文回答**

I'm interested in this role because it combines product design with real development work. My experience covers both electronics and accessories, and I enjoy working through the details that make a product possible. I was especially interested in your [product name] because of [one specific design detail]. I'd like to understand how your team makes those decisions. I believe my experience with materials, prototypes, and production could be useful for this role.

**練習提示：** 面試前補上公司的真實產品與一個具體觀察。不要只說「公司很有名」或臆測公司文化。

## 03. Why are you considering a new opportunity?

**中文題意：為什麼考慮轉換工作？**

**英文回答**

I've gained a wide range of experience at ASUS, from mobile accessories to gaming products. I'm grateful for the chance to work across different teams and product types. For my next step, I'd like to apply that experience to new design challenges and learn how another team develops products. I'm looking for a role where I can take responsibility for design decisions and stay involved through development and production.

**練習提示：** 這是正向動機範例，須符合實際離職原因。若被追問，補充真實背景，不需要批評現任公司。

## 04. What is your biggest strength as a designer?

**中文題意：你最大的設計優勢是什麼？**

**英文回答**

My biggest strength is connecting design ideas with production needs. I can explore the product's look and user experience, then work through materials and sample testing with engineers and suppliers. The fragrance mouse is a good example. Adding a scent feature also meant checking how the oils worked with the materials and internal parts. I stayed involved in those details and helped move the design toward production.

**練習提示：** 只選一項最強的能力，並立即用案例證明。不要只回答 creative、hardworking、team player。

## 05. What skill would you like to improve?

**中文題意：你還想加強哪一項能力？**

**英文回答**

One area I'd like to keep improving is laptop development. I have more years of experience in accessories, and my recent Vivobook work has helped me learn more about laptop production. I worked with manufacturers on 3D changes, mockups, and color approval. I want to build a deeper understanding of how design choices affect the whole laptop. My next step would be to join technical reviews earlier and learn more from the engineering team.

**練習提示：** 用真實成長方向回答，說明已有基礎及下一步。本題不把「太追求完美」當作缺點。

## 06. Walk me through your design process.

**中文題意：請說明你的設計流程。**

**英文回答**

I start by understanding who will use the product and what problem we need to solve. Then I study competing products and explore ideas through sketches and 3D models. Physical samples help me check things that are hard to judge on a screen, such as comfort and fit. During development, I work with product managers, engineers, and suppliers to review materials and production limits. I refine the design as we learn more from testing.

**練習提示：** 不只背流程名稱，要說每一步的目的。也可用香氛滑鼠串起整段回答。

## 07. How do you understand user needs?

**中文題意：你如何了解使用者需求？**

**英文回答**

I look at the situation in which people use the product. What are they carrying? How do they hold it? What feels difficult or uncomfortable? For the ERGO AIR backpack, we looked at carrying comfort, weight balance, and laptop protection. Samples and feedback from internal testers helped us improve the design. That feedback was useful, but it wasn't the same as a large user study. I'd want broader testing before making claims about all users.

**練習提示：** 區分使用情境觀察、內部測試及正式使用者研究，不把內部回饋誇大成大規模研究。

## 08. How do you use competitor research?

**中文題意：你如何運用競品分析？**

**英文回答**

I use competitor research to understand the choices users already have. I compare features, size, materials, and the way each product presents itself. Then I look for a need we could address more clearly. On the G22 project, market research and a review of earlier compact desktops helped shape the product direction. The goal was to find a clear reason for the product to exist and turn that into design choices.

**練習提示：** 強調比較後做了什麼決策，不必宣稱找到了市場上完全不存在的機會。

## 09. Tell me about the ASUS fragrance mouse.

**中文題意：請介紹 ASUS 香氛滑鼠。**

**英文回答**

The idea was to add a scent experience to an everyday computer mouse. My work included design concepts, hand-fit studies, 3D models, and physical samples. A key challenge was making sure the scent feature could work with the rest of the product. I worked with product managers, engineers, and suppliers to test oils, containers, and materials. We checked their compatibility with plastic parts and internal electronics, and continued to refine the design for production.

**練習提示：** 開頭說明產品價值，再談自己的工作與技術難點。不要只介紹產品功能。

## 10. How did you know the fragrance feature added value?

**中文題意：你怎麼知道香氛功能有價值，而不只是噱頭？**

**英文回答**

The idea was to offer a more personal experience in an everyday office product. However, a new feature needs evidence beyond being interesting. Our development work tested materials and the product's physical design. To judge long-term value, I'd also want to know whether people keep using the scent feature and whether it affects their buying decision. I would separate those questions from technical testing, because proving that a feature works doesn't prove that people want it.

**練習提示：** 本題考設計判斷。清楚區分「已做的驗證」與「還希望取得的使用者證據」。

## 11. Which project best shows your approach to design?

**中文題意：哪個專案最能代表你的設計方法？**

**英文回答**

The ROG ERGO AIR backpack is a strong example. We applied ideas from hiking backpacks to a gaming laptop bag, especially lighter materials and weight balance. My work covered early product direction and design development. We reviewed laptop protection and carrying comfort, then improved the design through samples and internal feedback. The project received 2024 iF and Red Dot design awards. It shows how I connect a clear user need with materials and product design.

**練習提示：** 獎項是專案成果。若你個人最有成就感的是別案，可以替换案例，保留回答結構。

## 12. What was your personal contribution to that project?

**中文題意：這個專案中，你個人到底負責什麼？**

**英文回答**

On ERGO AIR, my contribution included early product planning and design development. I worked on how the bag could balance laptop protection with lighter materials and carrying comfort. I also worked through samples and feedback as the design developed. The final product was a team effort with product managers and suppliers. I can explain the design work I handled, while giving the team credit for the parts we developed together.

**練習提示：** 作品集中準備一頁標示自己的範圍。若被追問某零件或決策，回答具體工作，不用籠統地說「全部」。

## 13. Tell me about the ROG G22 desktop.

**中文題意：請介紹 ROG G22 桌機專案。**

**英文回答**

The G22 project focused on a compact gaming desktop. We reviewed the market and problems with earlier models to help define its direction. My work covered design development from concepts and 3D models to mockups and production details. I also worked on CMF, lighting, packaging, and related parts. The challenge was keeping the product's design consistent as it moved through development. It gave me experience connecting the main product with the details around it.

**練習提示：** 不主張自己負責散熱工程或電子設計。設計整合與工程開發的責任要分清楚。

## 14. How do you design a product family?

**中文題意：你如何設計一個產品系列？**

**英文回答**

I first define what the products should share and where they need to be different. On the ARCHER backpack project, I led the planning of four bag styles for different uses. They needed a shared design language, but each bag also needed a clear purpose. I led workshops and guided the team through concepts and samples. We also planned future color and material updates so the family could continue to develop over time.

**練習提示：** 說出「共同識別」與「個別功能」的平衡，避免讓系列設計聽起來只是換尺寸與顏色。

## 15. Tell me about a difficult production problem you worked on.

**中文題意：請舉例說明你處理過的量產難題。**

**英文回答**

On the ALLY case project, I joined during later development. The existing 3D design needed changes before it could move into mold making and production. I revised the design, worked on CMF, and reviewed several rounds of samples. My role was to help turn the design into something that could be produced. This project shows why I stay involved after the concept stage. Important design work continues during sample reviews and production preparation.

**練習提示：** 若主管追問「究竟哪個地方不能量產」，請用你真實處理的零件或製程回答。履歷未載具體原因，本稿不預設壁厚、脫模角等問題。

## 16. How do you balance design quality and cost?

**中文題意：你如何平衡設計品質與成本？**

**英文回答**

I would first identify which parts of the design matter most to the user. Then I'd compare options with the engineer and supplier, including their cost and effect on the experience. In the Zenfone 3 Aura Flip Cover project, I worked with suppliers on soft materials and molding methods while considering cost and appearance. That experience taught me to explore production choices early, while there is still room to adjust the design.

**練習提示：** 有取捨邏輯即可，不必虛構降低成本的百分比。準備一個真實的材料比較作為追問內容。

## 17. How do you choose colors, materials, and finishes?

**中文題意：你如何做 CMF 決策？**

**英文回答**

I consider the user, the brand, and how the product will be made. A material needs to support the intended feel and work in actual use. I also want to see physical samples, because a rendering can't fully show texture or color. My experience includes plastic parts, soft materials, and bag fabrics. On Vivobook projects, I worked on color approval and production appearance, where small differences between samples can affect the final result.

**練習提示：** 重點是選材依據與實物驗證。可拿一個樣品比較故事補充，而不是只說「符合品牌」。

## 18. How do you test comfort and usability?

**中文題意：你如何驗證舒適度與易用性？**

**英文回答**

I use physical models to check the actions people need to perform. For a mouse, that includes hand fit and grip. For a bag, it includes carrying and reaching the contents. On the Zenfone flip cover project, I used models to check comfort when using the fingerprint reader. I would define the task before testing and record what needs to change. I also recognize that different users may need different sizes or adjustments.

**練習提示：** 前半段是已有案例，後半段是建議的方法表述。沒有正式測試樣本數時，不要自行補數字。

## 19. What would you do if an engineer said your design could not be made?

**中文題意：工程師說你的設計做不出來，你怎麼辦？**

**英文回答**

I'd ask which part creates the problem and what the limit is. Is it the material, the process, the space inside, or the cost? Then I'd explain the user need behind the design and work on alternatives with the engineer. I'd use a sample or a model to compare the options when possible. My goal would be to keep the important design value while finding a solution the team can build.

**練習提示：** 用 would 表達處理方式，不假裝曾發生一場具體衝突。可接 ALLY case 的開發經驗，但不捏造對話。

## 20. How would you handle disagreement with a product manager?

**中文題意：你與產品經理意見不同時，會如何處理？**

**英文回答**

I'd first check whether we disagree about the goal or the solution. The product manager may be focused on cost, timing, or market needs, while I'm looking at the user experience. I'd make those concerns clear and compare the options against the same criteria. If we still disagree, I'd suggest a focused test or review. Once a decision is made, I'd support the team and keep track of any remaining design risks.

**練習提示：** 核心是釐清分歧、共同標準、驗證及執行。若主管要求真實案例，換成實際發生過的事件。

## 21. How do you respond to critical design feedback?

**中文題意：你怎麼面對負面的設計回饋？**

**英文回答**

I'd try to understand the reason behind the feedback before defending the design. For example, “It doesn't feel right” could mean a problem with size, comfort, or brand identity. I'd ask for a more specific concern and compare it with the project goals. If the feedback points to a real issue, I'd revise the design. If I see a trade-off, I'd explain it with a model or clear comparison and ask for a decision.

**練習提示：** 表現開放，同時保留設計判斷。接受批評不代表每一條意見都直接照做。

## 22. Tell me about a design that did not work as expected.

**中文題意：請談一個沒有如預期發展的設計。**

**英文回答**

One development challenge I can discuss is the ALLY case. I joined after the initial design, and the existing 3D model wasn't ready for production. I revised it and worked through several rounds of samples. I wouldn't describe the original design as my own mistake, but the experience reinforced an important lesson: a convincing concept still needs production checks. In future projects, I'd bring those checks in earlier to help the team find problems sooner.

**練習提示：** 這是開發挫折案例，並非你的個人失誤。若主管明確問「你自己的錯誤」，務必準備另一個真實事件：當時判斷、影響、補救、改變。不要硬套本題。

## 23. How do you manage several projects at the same time?

**中文題意：多個專案同時進行時，你如何安排優先順序？**

**英文回答**

I'd compare deadlines, project risks, and what other people are waiting for. A sample review that blocks tooling may need attention before an early concept presentation. I'd break each project into clear next steps and confirm priorities with the team lead. If two deadlines conflict, I'd raise the issue early and explain the choices. My background covers several product types, so I'm familiar with projects that need very different kinds of design work.

**練習提示：** 重點是優先順序的判準，不只說自己會加班。此工作方法請依你的實際習慣調整。

## 24. How do you lead a design workshop and support other designers?

**中文題意：你如何帶領工作坊並協助其他設計師？**

**英文回答**

On the ARCHER backpack project, I led workshops and guided designers through concepts, samples, and production preparation. We needed four bags with different uses and a shared design language. I helped turn those needs into design directions and shared my knowledge of materials and manufacturing. For future workshops, I'd also make the expected output clear at the start and leave time to explain why each idea should move forward.

**練習提示：** 區分專案帶領與人事管理。不把工作坊經驗說成帶領正式直屬部門。

## 25. What makes you ready for a senior designer role?

**中文題意：你為什麼能勝任資深設計師？**

**英文回答**

I can take responsibility beyond the first design proposal. My experience includes product planning, materials, prototype reviews, and production support. I've also led a product family project and worked across design, engineering, product management, and suppliers. I understand that a senior designer needs to explain decisions and help the team move forward. I can contribute through hands-on design while sharing experience that helps others avoid problems during development.

**練習提示：** 用工作深度與責任證明資深，而不只是強調年資。準備 ARCHER 與 ALLY 兩個不同角色的例子。

## 26. How do you use AI in your design work?

**中文題意：你如何把 AI 用在設計工作中？**

**英文回答**

I've used generative AI to explore early ideas for laptop and handheld gaming projects, and I've shared methods with the team. It gives me more visual directions to discuss. I still need to judge whether those ideas fit the user, the brand, and the product limits. An AI image isn't a production-ready design. I'd take a useful idea into 3D development and physical testing before treating it as a workable solution.

**練習提示：** 說清楚 AI 用在哪個階段、由你做什麼判斷。工具名稱及使用熟練度請按實際情況補充。

## 27. How do you know if a design is successful?

**中文題意：你如何衡量設計成功？**

**英文回答**

I'd look at whether the design meets the user need, reaches the intended quality, and can be produced within the project limits. I'd also review user feedback and business results with the relevant teams. ERGO AIR received design awards, which recognized the project. However, awards are only one part of the picture. I'd still want to understand comfort in daily use, long-term quality, and customer response before judging the full result.

**練習提示：** 區分已知成果與希望追蹤的指標。不要把獎項直接當成銷量或滿意度證明。

## 28. How would you work with a global team?

**中文題意：你會如何與跨國團隊合作？**

**英文回答**

I'd use clear visuals and simple language to explain design decisions. Before a review, I'd share the goal, the options, and the questions we need to answer. Afterward, I'd confirm the decision, the owner of each task, and the next step in writing. If I didn't understand a comment, I'd ask for clarification. My experience working across design, engineering, and suppliers gives me a useful base for that kind of teamwork.

**練習提示：** 這是預計採用的合作方法，不主張未記載的跨國團隊領導經驗。能問清楚比假裝聽懂更重要。

## 29. What would you focus on in your first 90 days?

**中文題意：加入後的前 90 天，你會先做什麼？**

**英文回答**

In the first month, I'd learn about the users, the product line, and the team's process. I'd review current projects and understand the main design and production challenges. In the second month, I'd aim to contribute to a clearly defined project and build working relationships with the key teams. By the third month, I'd want to take responsibility for a design task and review my progress with my manager. I'd adjust this plan to the team's priorities.

**練習提示：** 強調了解現況後交付具體工作，不承諾三個月內完成整個產品上市。

## 30. Do you have any questions for me?

**中文題意：你有什麼問題想問我？**

**英文回答**

Yes, thank you. What is the most important design problem you want this person to help solve? I'd also like to understand how designers stay involved from early concepts through production. Finally, what would make you say, after six months, that the person in this role is doing well?

**練習提示：** 實際面談一次問一題，聽完再追問。三題分別了解職務任務、設計參與深度與績效期待。

## 練習安排

第一輪先練第 1、4、9、11、12、15、25、30 題，建立自介、代表作及個人貢獻的基本回答。第二輪練其餘題目，注意不要每題都使用同一個專案。

每題先看中文提示，用英文回答並錄音。第一次可看稿，第二次只看三個關鍵詞，第三次在不看稿的情況下回答。先把主旨講清楚，再補細節，不需要逐字背誦。

作品集旁準備三種證據：你的實際工作範圍、比較過的設計方案、樣品或模型如何影響決策。履歷未提供的測試人數、成本降低比例與銷售成果，請在確認前省略。

## 面試卡住時可用的短句

- **Could you rephrase the question, please?** 請換個方式說明問題。
- **Let me take a moment to think about that.** 讓我想一下。
- **Let me give you a specific example.** 我舉一個具體例子。
- **My part of the project was...** 我在這個專案負責的是……
- **That was a team decision. My contribution was...** 那是團隊決策，我的貢獻是……
- **I don't have the exact number, but I can explain what we tested.** 我沒有確切數字，但可以說明測試內容。
- **I haven't handled that exact situation, but my approach would be...** 我沒有處理過完全相同的情況，但我會……
- **Would you like me to go into more detail about the design or the production process?** 您想進一步了解設計，還是量產流程？
